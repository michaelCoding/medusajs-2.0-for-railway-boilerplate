# Frequently Asked Questions

## Orders & Shipping

### How do I track my order?

Log in to your account and visit the Orders section to see real-time tracking information.

### How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days. Express shipping is available at checkout.

## Returns & Refunds

### What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 30 days of purchase. Items must be in original condition.

### How do I initiate a return?

Contact our support team at support@example.com with your order number.

## Account

### How can I contact support?

Email us at support@example.com or use the contact form on our website.
